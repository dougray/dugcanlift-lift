LIFT is a nutrition and training tracker that works offline and keeps your data on your phone. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no cloud sync.

DASHBOARD
Opens on today: calories and macros against your goal, steps against a daily goal, what you've trained and what you've eaten. Below that, a week of stats and charts, and per-exercise progression showing your top weight and estimated one-rep max over your last ten sessions of any lift. Days you didn't log show as gaps, not zeros. Choose System, Light or Dark appearance.

MACRO CALCULATOR
Mifflin-St Jeor from your sex, age, weight, height and activity level. Pick lose, maintain or gain, a protein target and a fat target, and save the result as your goal.

FOOD LOG
Log food by weight in grams or ounces, and see what an entry will count as before you save it. Entries are grouped by meal with per-meal subtotals, and fibre is tracked alongside protein, fat and carbs. Search Open Food Facts or scan a barcode, enter anything by hand, re-log a recent food in one tap, and tap any entry to change it.

COOK
Write recipes, or paste a recipe's text - a blog post or a video caption - and edit the split into ingredients and method. Labelled macros per serving, and the finished dish's weight so a portion can be logged by weight. Plan recipes onto the week's meals, log a planned meal in one tap, and get the week's shopping added up into one list.

WORKOUT LOG
Sessions contain exercises, exercises contain sets, and each set records weight, reps, RPE, time and distance, all optional. A training focus - bodybuilding, powerlifting, CrossFit, Hyrox, endurance or everything - decides which fields you see without ever discarding data. Equipment is tracked separately, so a cable and a machine pulldown keep separate histories. A bundled library of 873 exercises, what you lifted last time under each exercise, and sets that prefill from the one before. Save routines in folders, or add starter routines in one tap: Push, Pull, Legs, Upper, Lower, Full Body, Mobility, Active Rest, Short Run and Long Run.

OUTDOOR
Record a run, walk or hike with GPS: time, distance, pace, elevation gain and the route, drawn as you go. Recording continues with the screen off, with a notification while it does. Review a finished activity and export it to Health Connect with its route. See your last route and personal bests for each activity type.

COACH
Send to Coach writes an email in your own email app with your log in a link: 4, 8 or 12 weeks or 6 months, daily food totals or every item, and your runs, walks and hikes. Your last route goes only if you turn it on, with its first and last 200 m cut off. A coach's plan link opens in the app to preview, then accept or decline.

YOUR DATA
Save a backup file - food, training, recipes, meal plan, and outdoor history with full GPS routes - wherever you choose, and restore it on this phone or another. Restoring only adds what the phone doesn't already have.

PRIVACY
Nothing leaves your phone unless you use a feature that sends it: a food search term or barcode goes to Open Food Facts when you search, and Send to Coach hands your log to your own email app. Full privacy policy at dugcanlift.com/app/privacy.
