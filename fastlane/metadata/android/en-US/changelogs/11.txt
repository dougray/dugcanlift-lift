Single-arm and single-leg work can now be logged left and right. Turn "Left and right separately" on for a lift — it is already on for the ones whose names say so — and each set records a side, alternating on its own so you only pick when you want to.

Exercise progression then charts each side on its own line, with how far apart they are and whether the gap is closing. A lift you have always logged two-sided is unchanged.

Your coach sees the same split, if you send them your log.
