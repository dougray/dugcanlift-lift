Single-arm and single-leg work can now be logged left and right. Turn "Left and right separately" on for a lift — it starts on for lifts whose names say so — and each set records a side, alternating on its own.

Exercise progression then charts each side on its own line. After three sessions a side it shows how far apart they are, and after four, whether the gap is closing. A lift logged two-sided is unchanged.

Your coach sees the same split, if you send them your log.
