Road Food: on a road trip, open Food and tap Road Food, pick the chain you're at or the gas station, and see what fits what you have left today — ranked by protein for the calories, with a short "a little over" list below it and one tap to log. It works offline and never asks where you are.

It covers Wendy's, Sonic, Chick-fil-A, Popeyes, Subway, Starbucks, Panera and QuikTrip, plus common gas station snacks. Every number comes from the chain's own published nutrition, and each chain shows when it was last checked.
